# Creative direction

Three concepts considered before implementation:

1. **The birthday trust** (selected). Engraved share certificates meet warm editorial design. Ivory feels like a keepsake, forest green gives financial gravitas, and restrained brass supplies celebration. An asymmetric headline/certificate hero leads into comparison rows and a personal letter. Cormorant Garamond brings ceremony; DM Sans keeps the interface crisp. Signatures: an engraved coin, certificate rules, and a wax-like seal. Motion: a quiet floating certificate followed by an expressive handoff. Avoid the usual fintech dashboard and centered marketing template.
2. **A future museum**. Swiss gallery typography, chalk white and ink black with vermilion details. A timeline frames the gift as an artifact from 2101. Monumental years, small accession labels, mechanically precise motion. Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono. Avoid card grids and financial chart wallpaper.
3. **The long game club**. Vintage sporting club stationery in butter yellow and burgundy. Offset ticket layout, scorecard-style stock choices, a hand-drawn ribbon, and tactile spring motion. Fraunces and Work Sans. Avoid a dark neon crypto aesthetic.

## Section structure

| Section | Structure | Focal point |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction | Editorial asymmetric | Neelesh's birthday and engraved share |
| Choice | Comparison columns, stacked on phones | Two selectable companies |
| Personal message | Pull quote strip | Harsha's signature |
| Terms | Prose block | The 75-year joke |
| Reveal | Full-screen stage, then certificate | Harsha handing the gift to Neelesh |

Warm, moderately sparse, personal copy with corporate detailing. Mobile is the base layout. Touch targets are at least 44px; the reveal supports safe areas, scrolling, keyboard dismissal, and reduced motion. Market numbers are fetched from public sources and always labeled with their time and source. The selection is a browser-local birthday keepsake; it does not execute a stock trade.
